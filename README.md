# Hi there.

I'm Nana, lovely to meet you! I'm a speech-language pathologist with a PhD in the field, a writer, and the creator and product lead of the [Sanapsis](https://www.sanapsis.com/) apps. I'm also the founder of Puheklinikka, a private speech and language clinic that specializes in adults who live with neurological conditions.

I love projects where clinical insight, research, and thoughtful product design come together to solve practical problems.

In my writing I combine Nordic clarity with Californian warmth. [Samples in my writing portfolio](https://nanalehtinen.github.io/Writing-voice-portfolio/).

## So what is on this profile?

I build tools for speech and language therapy and research. I also dabble in solutions for localization: one of my prototypes helps language experts turn what they know about their locale into a configuration for customer service AI agents.

The Sanapsis apps themselves are trilingual and live in private repositories: SanapsisPro for therapists, Sanapsis+ for clients practicing on their own, and the free Sanapsis Lite. The projects below are public.

## How do I use AI in development?

Glad you asked! For the first versions of Sanapsis, I hired and managed freelance developers. I owned everything else: the content, the requirements, the design, the testing and the releases.

Now I do the development myself, with AI assistance. I decide what the product needs to do and judge whether the result is right. Claude Code writes much of the code with me, and I also use ChatGPT, Gemini and Cursor. I review and test everything against real therapy and research use. This is how I rebuilt the original Cordova-based Sanapsis app, which had become hard to maintain and update.

Commits made with AI assistance carry a `Co-Authored-By` line, so you can always see where it was used.

## Research

My PhD (University of Turku, 2025) was a Finnish-English bilingual project on language attrition and spoken-language assessment. I designed and led it across California and Finland, and it produced a corpus of over 130 recorded sessions.

- Lehtinen, N., Kautto, A., & Renvall, K. (2024). Efficacy of clustering and switching strategies in verbal fluency tasks in a Finnish-English language attrition population. *The Language Learning Journal, 52*(2), 218–231.
- Lehtinen, N., Kautto, A., & Renvall, K. (2024). Frequent native language use supports phonemic and semantic verbal fluency in L1 and L2: An extended analysis of verbal fluency task performance in an L1 language attrition population. *International Journal of Bilingualism, 28*(5), 884–906.
- Lehtinen, N., Luotonen, I., & Kautto, A. (2023). Systematic administration and analysis of verbal fluency tasks: Preliminary evidence for reliable exploration of processes underlying task performance. *Applied Neuropsychology: Adult, 30*(6), 727–739.
- Dataset: [Spoken Finnish and English of Finnish-American Immigrants (amersuom)](https://urn.fi/urn:nbn:fi:lb-2024082921), Language Bank of Finland.
